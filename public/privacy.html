1. Who this policy covers

This Policy explains how GGHB Studio, the operator of the HUZHONG TIANJI (壶中天机) Daily Page website and web-based TikTok publishing integration (the “Service”), handles information associated with website use, account connection, media preparation, and authorized publishing.

The information handled depends on whether you simply visit the website or choose to connect an account and publish content. TikTok and hosting providers also process information under their own policies.

2. Information & permissions

  • Authorization information: TikTok account identifiers (such as an open ID), granted scopes, access and refresh tokens where issued, token expiration information, and connection status.
  • Basic profile, when needed and authorized: display name, avatar, and the identifiers returned under user.info.basic. This permission is only needed if the Service uses basic profile information to identify or display your account.
  • Publishing information: under video.publish, creator information needed to present permitted publishing options, such as account name/avatar, available privacy choices, interaction restrictions, and media limits; approved media, captions, settings, publish identifiers, status responses, and errors.
  • Operational information: hosting providers may process IP addresses, browser/device details, request times, URLs, and diagnostic logs to deliver and secure the website.
  • Requests you send us: contact-channel details, account identification, and the information you choose to include in support or privacy requests.

The TikTok authorization screen shows the actual permissions requested. Direct Post uses video.publish for supported photo or video publication. This integration does not require access to your TikTok password, private messages, contacts, or follower lists. These Terms do not authorize additional scopes or unrelated collection.

3. How information is used

Information is used to connect the correct account, maintain the authorized connection, show available posting settings, prepare and transmit approved content, check publishing results, troubleshoot failures, secure the Service, and respond to support or privacy requests.

Access is limited to what is needed for these functions. TikTok authorization data is not used for advertising, sold, or used to build unrelated profiles. Any new purpose or additional permission must be explained before the relevant collection or authorization.

4. Tokens & security

Access tokens allow API actions within the permissions you grant; refresh tokens, when issued, allow renewal of access. They are sensitive credentials. The Service’s handling requirements are to keep them in protected server-side storage, restrict access to authorized operators and processes, and exclude them from public HTML, browser code, media URLs, public GitHub repositories, and routine logs. Connections to TikTok and hosted pages use HTTPS.

Tokens are used only for the authorized integration and must be removed or invalidated when no longer needed, including after disconnection. Do not send tokens or passwords in support messages. No online service can guarantee absolute security; suspected unauthorized access should be reported through the contact route below.

5. Content & media processing

Media, captions, and publishing settings you approve are processed to prepare and submit a post to TikTok. TikTok may retrieve media from a verified hosted URL. Material placed at a public website or repository URL can be accessible to anyone with that URL and may be cached or copied. Do not place confidential media, credentials, or private account data in public hosting or source repositories.

TikTok processes submitted content under its own policies and your selected posting settings. Disconnecting the Service or deleting its working copy does not delete posts, copies, or records already held by TikTok.

6. Hosting, sharing & service providers

TikTok receives the account authorization and content information necessary to provide its API and publish approved posts. Netlify may deliver this website, public media, and any configured hosting functions. GitHub may store site source files and public media and support deployment. Use of these providers depends on the actual deployment; GitHub does not need to receive OAuth credentials to host the website’s source.

Information may also be handled by providers specifically needed to operate authorized publishing or by authorized operators responding to requests, only for the relevant Service purpose. Information may be disclosed where legally required or reasonably necessary to address fraud, security incidents, or protect rights. Providers may process information in countries other than yours.

Provider policies: Netlify Privacy Statement and GitHub General Privacy Statement.

7. Retention & minimization

Account connection information and tokens are retained only while needed for an active authorized connection. Temporary media and processing records should be removed when publishing, troubleshooting, or another stated operational need ends. Public Daily Page material may remain available as part of the site’s editorial archive.

Following disconnection or a verified deletion request, information no longer needed for the Service must be deleted. Limited records may be retained when required by law or needed for security or unresolved disputes, with access restricted to that purpose. Provider logs, caches, and backups may persist under the provider’s own retention practices. No fixed retention period or immediate removal from all backups is promised.

8. Your choices, revocation & deletion

You may choose not to connect TikTok, decline permissions, or stop using publishing features. Revoke the integration through TikTok’s settings for connected apps or app permissions. Revocation stops future authorized access; it does not undo completed posts.

To request access, correction, or deletion of information held by GGHB Studio, contact us through the channel through which you received access to the integration. Use the subject or opening line “Privacy request — HUZHONG TIANJI,” identify the request type, and provide your TikTok username or another account identifier sufficient to locate the connection.

We may request proportionate verification of account ownership before disclosing or deleting account data. Never provide your password or tokens. We will respond and handle requests within the time required by applicable law, explain any lawful limitations, and identify information that must be retained. Rights and complaint routes may vary by jurisdiction; you may contact the relevant privacy authority where available.

9. TikTok & other third-party policies

TikTok independently handles its platform accounts, authorization, posted content, and related records. Read TikTok’s Privacy Policy and use TikTok’s own controls for requests about information it holds. External links and provider services are governed by their respective policies.

These standalone policy pages do not add analytics scripts, advertising trackers, third-party fonts, or cookies. Hosting services may still process request information; any separate integration interface may use session mechanisms needed for authentication and must explain any additional tracking it introduces.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Policy to reflect changes to the Service or its data handling. The effective date will be updated here. Material changes should be communicated through an available Service or account communication channel where practicable. Additional TikTok permissions require a corresponding authorization flow; updating this Policy alone does not grant them.

11. Contact & privacy requests

Contact GGHB Studio through the communication channel through which you received access to the integration. Address the request to “GGHB Studio — HUZHONG TIANJI” and follow the privacy request instructions above.

Provide only the details necessary to locate your request. Do not share sensitive credentials. For the rules governing use of the Service, see our Terms of Service.
